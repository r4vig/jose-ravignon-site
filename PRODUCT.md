# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML + CSS + a little vanilla JS, no build step. Deployed on Vercel (framework "Other", output = root).

## Users

Three audiences, all non-technical decision makers:
- local shopkeepers and artisans (Seine-et-Marne / Île-de-France) who run things on paper or in Excel;
- shops that want to sell online (Shopify or fully custom);
- small companies that need an internal business application.

They arrive from a business card QR code, word of mouth or search, often on a phone, and decide whether to call or e-mail.

## Product Purpose

The public site of José Ravignon, independent web developer and designer. It presents custom websites, online shops and business applications, and gets the visitor to make contact (phone or e-mail). Success = a call or an e-mail about a project.

## Positioning

One person from first conversation to launch: the one who listens is the one who designs, builds and trains. Everything is custom, never a template resold to hundreds. Backed by day-to-day data-center work and cybersecurity training (hosting, security, backups done right).

- Name: José Ravignon. Wordmark "josé-ravignon.js".
- Visual world confirmed by the user (2026-10-22): keep the "code editor" identity (tabs, code window, syntax colours, amber accent, status bar) and elevate it.
- Fonts: Manrope + JetBrains Mono, self-hosted.
- Voice: French, plain words, "vous", reassuring, no jargon pushed on the reader.

## Evidence on Hand

- Three real projects described in text only: perfume shop redesign (in progress), stock & sales tracking app, online parts catalogue.
- No photos, screenshots, logo, testimonials or client names. Never invent any of them.

## Product Principles

1. The visitor is not technical: every technical element is decoration or explanation, never a barrier.
2. Contact must be one tap away at all times.
3. Show the custom mechanism, don't just claim it.
4. Honesty over hype: no invented numbers, clients or reviews.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard use, reduced-motion respected, readable on small phones.
